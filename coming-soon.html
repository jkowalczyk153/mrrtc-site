Connecting communities. Building the future.

We’re working to transform over 100 miles of historic railroad corridors into a connected network of trails that brings people and communities together. Places to explore, enjoy the outdoors, and experience the beauty of the Monadnock Region.

There’s work to be done. And we’re making progress.

Mile by mile, bridge by bridge, we’re making our regional trail network a reality. We’ve accomplished a lot, but there’s much more to do.

Get involved

Get involved. We need you!

These trails are for all of us. Whether you donate, volunteer, or help spread the word, you can be part of completing a trail network that will benefit our communities for generations to come.

Meet the board

Michael Kowalczyk

President and founder

Read bio

Mike moved to the Monadnock Region in 2001 and for 17 years bicycled to work on a regular basis. After retiring in 2017 and moving to Swanzey, Mike helped establish the Swanzey Rail Trail Advisory Committee which improves and maintains the 11 miles of rail trail in Swanzey.

In 2019 Mike founded the Monadnock Region Rail Trail Collaborative whose mission is to promote the reconstruction, rehabilitation, maintenance, and use of the Monadnock Region’s ~100-mile rail trail network.

In 2020 Mike joined the New Hampshire Rail Trail Coalition board helping to advocate for the rail trails at the state level. Mike is a member of the Rails-To-Trails Conservancy.

Henry Underwood

Secretary

Read bio

Henry is a Senior Planner at the Southwest Region Planning Commission (SWRPC) in Keene, where he has worked for more than a decade helping the region’s 34 communities with transportation, GIS mapping, and long-range planning.

At SWRPC, Henry has worked on several rail trail planning projects, including the Ashuelot Rail Trail plan, which helped spark interest in a regional collaborative, and the Cheshire Rail Trail South Planning Project. He also supports the Monadnock Alliance for Sustainable Transportation (MAST) and its Complete Streets program, which helps towns create safer places to walk and bike.

Henry joined the MRRTC board in 2022 and serves as Secretary. For the Collaborative, he built an interactive map of rail trail projects and set up its new email and file-sharing system. Look for Henry walking or biking on the Cheshire Rail Trail in Troy, where he lives.

Laura Susmann

Board member

Read bio

Laura moved to the Monadnock Region after college. She has enjoyed horseback riding on local rail trails. She has volunteered with several area organizations to maintain various stretches of the rail trails.

Laura is an active member of the New Hampshire Horse Council, New England Horse and Trail Association and the Arabian Horse Association. She is also a council member with the Friends of Pisgah State Park.

Laura joined the MRRTC in 2026 to offer the equestrian point of view regarding the rail trails.

Bob Meissner, Jr.

Board member

Read bio

Bob Meissner, Jr. has spent 50 years carving out careers across six fields—recruiting, banking, franchising, international business, community leadership, and teaching—holding 20 different roles. Fluent in Spanish and traveled widely, Bob earned an M.A. from Middlebury Graduate School.

A passionate speaker, career counselor, and youth motivator, Bob has volunteered with nearly 20 nonprofits. An MRRTC biker, father of two professionals, and married to an artist and animal activist. Bob has lived in the Monadnock Region for 25 years in four different towns.
