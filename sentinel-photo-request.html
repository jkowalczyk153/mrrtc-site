About us and this request

The Monadnock Region Rail Trail Collaborative (MRRTC) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2019. We work with towns, Cheshire County, snowmobile clubs, trail groups and volunteers to rebuild, maintain, connect and promote more than 100 miles of rail trails in southwestern New Hampshire.

We are building a new website and interactive trail map. We want photographs of real people enjoying our trails and of the work that keeps them going. We are not asking you to shoot anything new. We would like to know whether the Sentinel has existing photographs we could license, and on what terms.

The five trails

Our network has five trails: Cheshire, Ashuelot, Fort Hill, Monadnock, and Peterborough to Hillsborough. The map shows how far the rebuilding has come.

What we are looking for

  • People and activities: families with children, older adults, walkers, hikers, runners, dog walkers, bicyclists, equestrians, snowmobilers, snowshoers and Nordic skiers.
  • Places: any of the five trails and the towns they connect, including bridges, wetlands, forests, trailheads and downtown Keene.
  • Seasons: spring, summer, fall and winter.
  • Community and projects: trail openings and ribbon cuttings, volunteer work days, bridge and surface work, and races, rides or walks on or near the trails.
  • The people shown: we hope the set reflects the region's real makeup plus visible variety in age, ability, background and family type.
  • Format: wide horizontal images are especially useful for website banners, but any orientation helps.

What we would like from you

  1. A short description of the Sentinel's archive of trail, outdoor recreation and community photographs, and how far back it goes.
  2. A preview of relevant images, as a contact sheet or private gallery link. For each image, please give the date, location, activity and photographer.
  3. Who owns the rights to each image: Sentinel staff, freelancers or contributors, or a wire service. We cannot use wire images.
  4. Any limits on use, including for images of identifiable people or children, and for promotional or fundraising use.
  5. Your pricing and license terms, as described below.

Rights we need

For each image we license, we ask for a non-exclusive license that lasts as long as you can offer, with these terms:

  • Use by MRRTC and anyone working on its behalf (our volunteer web team, hosting providers, designers, printers and grant writers) with no separate approval.
  • Use on our website, interactive map, social media, newsletters, annual reports, grant applications, fundraising and print materials.
  • Cropping, resizing and color correction. We will not alter people or scenes with AI.
  • Your credit line, if you require one.

Pricing

  • Please give a price per image and any package price for a set (for example 10, 25 or 50 images).
  • Tell us if you offer a nonprofit or community-partner rate.
  • Tell us whether fees are one-time or recurring, and how long your prices hold.

Delivery

Original files at the highest resolution available, with caption, date, location and credit in the file information, sent by download link.

Optional: new photography

If Sentinel photographers take outside assignments, please tell us your rates and lead time. This is optional, and we are not committing to any commissioned work.

How we will decide

We will look at how well your images fit our list, how clear the rights are, price, and how quickly you can deliver. We may license from more than one source, and we may license only part of what you offer. Lowest price will not decide the outcome.

Responses are due [date to be set by MRRTC]. Questions go to [MRRTC contact to be confirmed].
